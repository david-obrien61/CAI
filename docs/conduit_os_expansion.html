Competitive Landscape Gap

The dominant software in HVAC field service — ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber — starts at $199–$599/month and requires weeks of setup. They target companies with 5+ trucks and a dedicated office manager.

The 1–3 truck owner-operator is completely underserved. That is Conduit OS's exact target — the same profile as the auto shops in the Ignition OS pilot.

The Sales Motion Is Identical

Cold call the owner. Walk into the shop or dispatch office. Leave the hardware kit. Show the margin gap on their own numbers. The pitch hook changes from "what you charge vs. what your slab recommends" to "what you charge vs. what your parts actually cost you."

Same RAM mount. Same Anker cable. Same 14-day trial. Same $38 CAC.

Adapt — Re-skin / Re-label / Re-prompt

Job Intake → Service Ticket Intake 4–6 hrs
What changes: "Vehicle" → "Equipment/Unit", VIN decode → Equipment model lookup (AHRI number, serial, install date, location/address). Same intake flow, same status engine, different field labels and AI prompts.
New field: Service address (HVAC goes to the customer's location — auto shops don't).
PMI Engine → Maintenance Schedule Manager 3–4 hrs
What changes: PMI tasks re-prompted for HVAC (filter changes, coil cleaning, refrigerant check, belt inspection) vs. auto (oil changes, brake checks). The scheduling engine, override logic, and Supabase schema are identical. Only the default task templates change.
Fleet / HUB → Dispatch Board 6–8 hrs
What changes: "Trucks" and "fleet units" become "technician routes" with service addresses as destinations. The GPS ping infrastructure already exists (driver_lat/lng in POs). Enhance the map view, add route optimization display, real-time tech status. The HUB module is ~70% of this already.
Admin Dashboard (OmniDashboard) → Conduit Command 4–5 hrs
What changes: KPI tiles re-labeled (jobs in bay → calls dispatched, RO count → tickets closed, bay utilization → tech utilization). New KPI: maintenance agreements active. Color palette shifts from Ignition blue-slate to Conduit green-slate. Brand assets only — zero logic changes.
Onboarding Wizard → Conduit Setup Wizard 3–4 hrs
What changes: Questions re-worded for HVAC context (trade type: HVAC/Plumbing/Electrical, number of trucks, EPA certification status). Onboarding path logic identical — MARGIN path, DIAGNOSE path, MIGRATE path all still apply.
Voice Command Module → Conduit Voice 2–3 hrs
What changes: Voice command prompts re-written for HVAC terminology ("log refrigerant charge", "add filter to parts", "mark coil cleaning complete"). Whisper transcription and Claude reasoning layers unchanged — only the command-matching prompts update.
Invoice Audit (Gemini Vision) → Equipment Inspection 3–4 hrs
What changes: In Ignition OS, Gemini reads invoices. In Conduit OS, Gemini reads equipment photos — age estimation from unit label, refrigerant type identification, damage assessment, nameplate data extraction. Same Gemini vision call, completely different prompt and output schema.

New Build — Net-New Tiles for Conduit OS

★ Equipment Registry 8–10 hrs
What it is: A database of every HVAC unit, boiler, water heater, or panel the contractor has ever serviced — linked to a customer address. Think of it as the "vehicle database" equivalent.
Fields: Equipment type, make/model/serial, install date, refrigerant type, warranty expiry, service address, assigned tech, service history.
AI hook: Gemini reads the nameplate photo on first visit — auto-populates the registry. Zero data entry for the tech.
★ Refrigerant Tracking (EPA 608 Compliance) 8–12 hrs
What it is: EPA Section 608 requires certified technicians to log every refrigerant purchase, recovery, and charge. Current tracking is paper or spreadsheet — creating significant compliance risk.
CAI value: Every service ticket automatically prompts refrigerant logging. Monthly summary report for EPA records. Certificate expiry reminders for techs.
The close: "An EPA violation fine starts at $44,539 per day per violation. Our log costs $299/month."
★ Maintenance Agreement Manager 10–14 hrs
What it is: HVAC contractors' most valuable revenue stream — annual service agreements ($150–300/unit/year) that guarantee two visits. Most small contractors track these in a notebook or not at all.
CAI value: Create agreements, auto-schedule PM visits, send renewal reminders, track agreement revenue vs. one-off revenue. Dashboard shows monthly recurring revenue (MRR) — a metric most shop owners have never seen.
The hook: 20 agreements at $200/year = $4,000 guaranteed revenue before opening the doors in January.
★ Parts Van Inventory 6–8 hrs
What it is: Unlike a shop where parts are in a stock room, HVAC techs carry $5,000–20,000 in parts on their trucks. Tracking what's on which truck, what got used, what needs restocking is a daily pain point.
CAI value: Each tech has a van inventory profile. Parts used on a ticket auto-deduct from van stock. Low-stock alerts trigger reorder. Cross-referenced to Margin Engine for true cost-of-goods-sold.
★ Permit Tracker 4–6 hrs
What it is: HVAC system replacements and electrical work require building permits in most jurisdictions. Tracking permit status, inspection dates, and final sign-off is currently done on whiteboards or sticky notes.
CAI value: Link a permit to a job ticket. Log application, approval, inspection, and close dates. Alert when a permit is approaching expiry without final sign-off.
★ Energy Efficiency Report (Customer-Facing) 5–6 hrs
What it is: After each visit, Claude generates a plain-English efficiency report for the homeowner: equipment age, SEER rating vs. current standards, estimated energy cost vs. new system cost, recommendation.
The close: This is the replacement upsell tool. A homeowner who sees "your 2009 unit costs you ~$180/month more to run than a new SEER 18 system" makes the decision themselves. The tech doesn't have to sell.
AI hook: Claude generates the report from the service ticket data. Gemini reads the nameplate for the spec data.

Why subdomains, not separate domains, for the apps

One Railway project can serve multiple subdomains. One SSL certificate covers *.builtwithcai.com. One deployment pipeline. As you add Anchor OS, FieldOS, and future verticals — each gets a subdomain, zero new infrastructure cost.

Why keep the product .com domains

When you cold call a HVAC contractor, you hand them a card that says conduit-os.com. Not builtwithcai.com/conduit. The product domain is for sales. The platform domain is for investors. Both point at the same place.
