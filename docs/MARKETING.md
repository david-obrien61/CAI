# Ignition OS — Go-To-Market & Marketing Plan

## Two Stories, One Product

Ignition OS has two audiences and two pitches. Know which room you're in.

| Audience | One-Line Pitch | Close With |
|---|---|---|
| **Shop owner / pilot** | "Stop losing margin on parts, tools, and labor. 14 days free, no card." | Savings number from their own jobs |
| **Investor / partner** | "We built a composable AI platform proven in the hardest SMB vertical. Auto shops are the beachhead." | Vertical expansion roadmap + architecture reuse story |

Never lead with the platform story to a shop owner. They don't care. Lead with the dollar they're losing today.

## The Hardware Kit ("The Ignition Starter Pack")

Leave this at the shop on day one of the pilot. Cost is your customer acquisition cost.
**Target kit cost: under $50 per shop (without printer loaner)**

| Item | Recommended Model | Purpose | Est. Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Magnetic toolbox mount | **RAM Mounts magnetic base** (neodymium magnet, no adhesive needed — attaches to any steel toolbox surface) | Phone always at workstation, always on app | ~$25 |
| Charging cable | **Anker PowerLine III Flow USB-C** (kevlar-reinforced, 35,000 bend rating) | Survives shop abuse. Eliminates "my phone died" excuse | ~$10 |
| Printed voice command cheat sheet | Print + laminate locally | Taped to toolbox — "Ignition, Clock In" etc. | ~$1 |
| Branded sticker / decal | Custom order (Sticker Mule min qty) | Logo on the mount — visible every day | ~$2 |

**Total leave-behind kit: ~$38/shop**

### Why RAM Mounts — Not MagSafe
MagSafe only works reliably with iPhone 12+. RAM Mounts are universal (any phone, any case), used in military vehicles, police cruisers, and EMS equipment. The neodymium magnet base snaps onto any steel toolbox surface — no drilling, no adhesive. If the shop is hard on equipment, the RAM mount survives it. The tech's phone can have any case, any brand.

### The Barcode Printer Strategy

**For cold calls / demo:** Bring one **Brother QL-820NWB** (~$200) as your demo unit.
- WiFi + Bluetooth — prints wirelessly from the app, no computer needed
- 110 labels/min — fast enough to label a whole shop's tools in one visit
- LCD screen — can print standalone without a phone if needed
- The demo: snap a photo of a tool label → AI reads it → tool added to inventory → barcode prints in 3 seconds. That's the close.

**For pilot shops (loaner model):** Leave a **Brother QL-800** (~$120) with converting shops.
- USB only (simpler, less to go wrong)
- 93 labels/min — plenty for daily use
- Label rolls: ~$15 for 400 labels (DK-2205 continuous roll works for barcodes)
- Once 20+ tools are labeled and PMI schedules running, removing it means losing all that history
- After trial converts: shop buys their own. You get the QL-800 back for the next prospect.

**After conversion:** Recommend they purchase the **QL-820NWB** for wireless freedom (~$200). Their call.

## The Pitch Flow

### Step 1 — The Cold Walk-In (or call)
**Target:** Service writer or owner, not the tech
**Opening line:** *"We built shop management software specifically for diesel shops in the Leander area. We're doing a regional pilot — 14 days free, full access, no card. Can I show you one thing?"*
**The one thing:** Pull up the margin engine. Ask what they charge for a $45 part. Show them what the slab engine would price it. The gap is the hook.

### Step 2 — The Leave-Behind
Drop the starter kit. Mount the charger on a toolbox in their presence.
*"This stays here. If you hate the app after 14 days, keep the charger."*

### Step 3 — Day 12 Follow-Up
The app auto-generates the savings report.
Call or text: *"Ignition flagged $[X] this week. Want me to walk you through it before the trial ends?"*

### Step 4 — The Close
Show the savings number vs. the subscription cost.
$299/mo Professional vs. $450/mo in recovered margin = easy math.

## Conversion Mechanics (Trial → Paid)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Kit installed, trial starts, shop gets full PREMIER |
| Day 7 | In-app nudge: "X jobs processed, $Y in margin tracked" |
| Day 12 | Savings report auto-generated + owner gets a call |
| Day 14 | Trial expiry warning — tiles gray out at midnight |
| Day 15 | Data visible but blurred, subscribe button prominent |
| Day 30 | Data archived (not deleted) — retrieval triggers subscription |

**The psychological play:** The shop has real data in the system by day 14.
Losing that data feels worse than paying $299/mo.

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Shop-Ware / Mitchell1 / Tekmetric (existing shop software)
- They charge $300–600/mo for a rigid, monolithic system
- No AI, no voice, no composable blocks
- Designed for the office, not the toolbox
- **Our angle:** "Same price, but the AI does the work and the software fits the shop — not the other way around."

### vs. Generic AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini)
- General purpose — shop owner has to know what to ask
- No integration with their jobs, parts, or vendors
- No mobile kiosk for techs
- **Our angle:** "Ignition OS knows what a diesel shop needs. You don't have to explain it."

### vs. Enterprise fleet software (Samsara, Geotab)
- $1,000+/mo, requires hardware purchase, 12-month contracts
- Built for fleets of 50+, not the 3-truck shop
- **Our angle:** "Fleet-grade tracking at 1/10th the price with no hardware required."

## The Composable Moat (Why They Can't Leave)

Once a shop is running, leaving means:
1. Losing all job history and customer records
2. Losing all barcoded tool inventory and PMI schedules
3. Losing the AI that knows their margin rules and labor rates
4. Physically removing the hardware kit from every toolbox

**This is by design.** Every piece of value added during the trial makes leaving more expensive than staying.
